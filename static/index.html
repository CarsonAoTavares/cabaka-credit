How it works

The problem. One hospital visit can block a family from owning a home: unpaid medical bills go to collections, the credit score drops, and the mortgage is denied. Meanwhile, small mortgages (<$150k) cost lenders about as much to originate as big ones, so few banks make them.

One agentic rail, two products. The same four agents handle a medical loan at the clinic and a small mortgage at a community bank. Only the policy and tool set change.

  1. Gatekeeper (Claude Haiku): screens the free-text notes for prompt injection, plus a regex pre-filter.
  2. Underwriter (Claude Opus): calls cash-flow, credit, benchmark and eligibility tools; recommends a max affordable payment.
  3. Offer Architect (Claude Opus): builds and compares options with exact calculators, including the deferred-interest trap and the effect on mortgage readiness.
  4. Compliance & Explainer (Claude Opus): checks policy and fair lending, writes a plain-language letter in the borrower's language.
  5. Verifier (plain code): re-computes the math, checks every $ and % in the letter against tool outputs, enforces payment and APR caps, blocks prompt leaks.

Guards. Agents can only read the one applicant file being reviewed; max 8 turns per agent; per-run token cap; daily dollar budget; per-visitor rate limit. A human loan officer always makes the final decision.

All applicants are fictional. Benchmarks marked "illustrative" are demo values. This is a prototype, not a lending offer.
