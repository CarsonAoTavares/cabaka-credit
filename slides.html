The problem: one broken pipeline

43M

credit reports carry medical collections, a poor predictor of repayment (CFPB 2022)

30% vs 19%

rural vs urban buyers denied for credit history (Pew, HMDA 2018–23)

$11.6k

to originate a mortgage (Freddie Mac, Q3 2023), so a $112k loan loses the lender money

Clinic offers a 0% card → one slip = retroactive 26.99% interest → collections → score drops → mortgage denied.
The 2025 CFPB rule removing medical debt from credit reports was vacated in July 2025.

What's new

Today
CareCredit: score-based, deferred-interest trap.
PayZen: cash-flow plans, but blind to housing.
Blend/nCino: built for big loans.
ChatGPT: does math in its head, injectable.
Cabaka
Every medical offer is scored by "does the family stay mortgage-ready?"
One agentic rail, two products: clinic loans + small mortgages.
Cash-flow + medical-excluded score.
Code verifies every number.

Safety: defenses that actually fire

  • Prompt injection → Gatekeeper
  • Cross-applicant data → ID lock
  • Invented numbers → Verifier
  • Predatory offers → APR + payment caps
  • Runaway loops → 8-turn cap
  • Overspend → $5/day + rate limit
  • 17 automated tests pass
  • Live red-team run: no $50k loan, no leak
